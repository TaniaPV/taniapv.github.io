International Day of Women and Girls in Science

Participation in a roundtable discussion organised by the University of Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (ULPGC) to mark the International Day of Women and Girls in Science.
February 2026.

Marine science outreach in secondary schools

Outreach talks and interactive activities introducing marine sciences and Southern Ocean research to secondary school students, through activities associated with COUPLING II and GIEMAR, the Educational Innovation Group in Marine Sciences (GIE-66, ULPGC).
Academic years: 2024–2025 and 2025–2026.

Feria Internacional del Mar (FIMAR)

Collaboration in interactive information and science outreach activities, and delivery of the talk “Las profundidades de la Antártida: un poderoso motor para el clima global”.
Organisers: Institución Ferial de Canarias (INFECAR) and Cabildo de Gran Canaria.
Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, Spain · June 2024.
