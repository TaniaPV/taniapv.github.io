Skills

Programming and scientific computing

Advanced MATLAB programming and working experience with Python for oceanographic data processing, visualisation, analysis of large multidimensional datasets, and quantitative diagnostics. Technical expertise includes transport calculations, velocity-field analysis, frontal detection, Lagrangian particle-tracking experiments, climatological analyses, model–observation comparison, and assessment of spatio-temporal variability in gridded ocean products.

Observational data processing

Processing and quality control of hydrographic, velocity, underway, autonomous, and satellite datasets, including CTD, LADCP, SADCP, XBT, SEASOAR, drifter data, satellite altimetry, and satellite-derived surface products such as chlorophyll-a fields. Experience with ship-based and post-cruise workflows, including hydrographic and velocity data preparation for scientific analysis and comparison with gridded products.

Ocean model and reanalysis output analysis

Analysis and interpretation of eddy-resolving reanalysis and model outputs, including NEMO- and HYCOM-based systems, to evaluate circulation, transport, frontal structure, water-mass properties, and temporal variability. Experience using numerical products in combination with in situ and satellite observations for model–data comparison, process-oriented diagnostics, and assessment of the physical mechanisms controlling connectivity and ecosystem-relevant variability.
